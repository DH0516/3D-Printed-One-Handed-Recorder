# 5. How to Assemble

1. **Preparing the Body & Tone Holes:** 
   
   - Inspect the printed bore and tone holes for any stringing, burrs, or layer seam bumps.
   
   - Clean tone hole perimeters using fine needle files or 600-grit sandpaper, ensuring smooth seating rims for key pads.
   
   - Lightly sand the male tenons and female sockets with 400-grit sandpaper until parts test-fit smoothly without binding.
   
   - Optionally apply a thin coat of food-grade oil (flax, or similar used in woodwind oiling) to the internal bore for improved sealing.

2. **Mounting the Keywork:** 
   
   - Test-fit each printed key arm into its corresponding pivot pillar/post on the recorder body.
   
   - Insert a standard 1.75mm 3D printing FDM filament (or applicable pins) through the pillar posts and pin holes, that will be acting as a pin spring.
   
   - Rotate the filament pin in the direction that will apply its appropriate sealing force. 
   
   - Snap off the filament at the notch to ensure that the filament pin spring will stay in one rotational position.

3. **Applying & Checking Pad Seals:** 
   
   - Punch or cut circular pad discs (1.0–1.5 mm closed-cell neoprene, natural cork, or similar) matching the key cup diameter.
   
   - Apply a small drop of glue or double-sided tape inside the key cup, then press the pad disc firmly into place.
   
   - After assembly, check seal airtightness: gently press the already closed key over its covering tone hole and play the note to see if there is sign of weak note, with no hiss or leakage.

4. **Final Joint Fitting & Testing:** 
   
   - Wrap the tenon joints with cork sheet, thread, paper tape, or PTFE tape until a firm, airtight friction fit is achieved.
   
   - Apply a light touch of cork grease or similar lubricant if needed.
   
   - Align a commercial soprano head joint with the body tone holes, insert the foot joint (similar tenon taping needed).

### Design Customizations for Tenons:

- **Grooves for Threads:** If enough users request it, the author will add versions of the model files with pre-cut grooves designed specifically for thread wrapping. Note that this is not a standard design for recorders.
- **O-Ring Grooves:** Some have suggested O-ring seals. If there is sufficient interest, there will be a version featuring O-ring groove joints.
- **Modifying & Sharing:** You are welcome to download the design files and edit them to fit your specific needs. If you make modifications and wish to share them with the community for this instrument, send your designs to the author to be posted on the project site.
