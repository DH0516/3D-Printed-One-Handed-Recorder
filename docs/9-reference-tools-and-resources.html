8. Reference Tools & Resources

  • Interactive Fingering Chart Viewer: A dedicated web application to view and practice the fingerings, laid out for the one-handed mechanism. The chart itself is the standard baroque chart ("English" and not the "German" system), since the keys change which finger works a hole rather than which holes are open.
  • Features: Three note-naming modes (Simple, Solfège, Standard); Simple and Solfège number the instrument's own octaves (C1 to C#3, Do 1 to Di 3), Standard keeps scientific names (C5 to C#7). The playing range is chosen at a reasonable random for people who would require this when learning the instrument as a beginner. Staff notes are drawn one octave lower to match the instrument's register. Plus search filtering and print/PDF of the current view.
  • Standalone Use: Fully static; it reads the model list and each model.json from the models/ directory, so serve the folder on any static host (GitHub Pages included) and open it from there.
  • Model A1 Documentation: Specifications and print files for the standard soprano build; printing, assembly, and troubleshooting are in chapters 4 to 6.
  • Acoustic Modeling: Inria Openwind, the open-source wind instrument design toolbox used to calculate and optimize the bore and toneholes.
  • Design Compliance and History: Historical background on one-handed woodwinds (including expired patents) can be found in Background & Inspiration. Open-source licensing and IP compliance details are listed in Project and Instrument Details and Contexts.
  • Community Support: In these early stages of release, we welcome educators, makers, and families reaching out to us for assembly advice, troubleshooting, and collaborative feedback.
